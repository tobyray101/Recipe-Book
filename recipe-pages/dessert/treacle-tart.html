Treacle Tart

Home

About This Recipe

This treacle tart recipe is a great dessert that impresses all at a party. It has a lovely sweet and sticky filling made from bread and golden syrup, encased in a buttery shortcrust pastry. Perfect when served warm with a dollop of cream or a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Golden Syrup
  • 62.5 Grams Butter
  • 75 Grams Margirine
  • 300 Grams Plain Flower
  • 296 ML Water

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to make the pastry, this is much easier with a mixer however, still possible without it just may take more skill and time. Mix 62.5g of butter, 62.5g of margirine and 227g of plain flour.
  2. 2. Mix in untill it becomes a breadcrumby look, then slowly add water mixing it in, untill you get a pastry dough ball.
  3. 3. Wrap the dough in cling film and place in the fridge for at least 30 minutes.
  4. 4. Nect make the treacle mix. You want to blend up your bread amd place into a mixing bowl. Mix in golden syrup untill it is fully covered but not liquidy. It should go a nice golden colour with a thick consistency.
  5. 5. Wipe the inside of your tart tin with the margirine and a pastry brush untill it is all covered in a light layer. Then lightly dust with some flour.
  6. 6. Scatter flour in your work surface, place on the dough and push down with your hand to prepare it for the rolling pin.
  7. 7. Roll the pastry out, pushing sides in to keep them strait and rotating when needed. Keep rolling untill its wider then the tin with some overhang big enough to go up the sieds of the tin.
  8. 8. To push the patry in to the tin, gently lift up the outside and push down in the corner with your knuckles, this should push the air out and make sure theres no bubbles, continue this all around the circumference of the tin.
  9. 9. Scoop the treacle mix into the pastry and use a fork to push it down into the corners and cover the whole bottom.
  10. 10. Use a knife around the top edge of the tin to remove all the extra pastry.
  11. 11. Pre heat the oven to 140 degrees celcius, place it in and bake for 50 minutes.
  12. 12. Once cooked, take it out and let it cool before removing from the tin and serving.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • When putting the treacle mix into the tart, fill it close to the top of the pastry but leave some room because it will rise.

  • A tart tin with a push up-able bottom is usefull for this recipe, and use a small knife to cut the tart of if its stuck after cooking.

  • The treacle mix will last a while once mixed so use spare bread thats almost out of date to maximise useage and decrease waste.
