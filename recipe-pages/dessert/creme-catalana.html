About This Recipe

This creme catalana recipe is a homemade zesty custard dessert topped with a layer of glass like caramel. The way the caramel layer shatterts when you dig your spoon into it, and the mix of creamy soft caremel with hard sweet shards of careml works great together.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Double Cream
  • Cinamon Stick
  • Orange
  • Lemon
  • Egg
  • Caster sugar
  • Cornflower

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to make a caramel powder. To do this you want to pour caster sugar into a pan and heat on low heat and mix around to melt and caramelize the sugar. Once it becomes a liquid, take off the heat and pour onto non stick surface and leave to cool and harden.
  2. 2. Pour 200ml of milk, 75ml of double cream, and a cinamon stick into a pan and then zest an orange and lemeon into it. Stiring often, cook for 4 minutes untill its hot but not boiling. Take off the heat and let it rest for 15-30 minutes.
  3. 3. Whisk 3 large or 4 medium egg yolks with 50g of caster sugar and 22g of cornflower. Then pour your milk/ cream mixture passing it through a sive into the bowl of egg mix and mix.
  4. 4. Pour into a pot and head over a medium high heat for 12 minutes or untill a custard like consistency. Pass this mixture through a sive and into your ramekin. Let it sit and cool.
  5. 5. Take your caramel from step 1 and crush pr blend untill it becomes a powder.
  6. 6. Sprinkle a layer of caramel powder over your custard, it should be evenly covering the whole top. Place under a grill or use a blow tourch to re-caramelise.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Pour your egg from 1 half of the shell to the other to seperate the yolk from the whites.

  • You can use baking paper to stop the caramel sticking when trying to cool.

  • Using the caramel powder instead of sugar on to of your custard means it will caramelise quicker so the custard wont get too hot or burn before the sugar carmelises.
