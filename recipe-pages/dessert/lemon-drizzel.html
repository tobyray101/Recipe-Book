Lemon Drizzle Cake

Home


About This Recipe

This recipe uses a basic cake recipe with a small addition of lemon zest then pieced and infused with mnore lemon juice and jest. This cake is light and fluffy but still full of flavour. Its then covered in a zesty icing glaze, drizzeled on.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack butter
  • 3lbs self raising flour
  • 1 1/2 caster sugar
  • 1 pint milk
  • 12 eggs
  • Icing sugar
  • Lemon

How To Make

  1. 1. Measure out flour and sugar and add to a bowl, cut your butter into cubes and add, crack your eggs onto the bowl. Measure out 1 pint of milk, start mixing and add the milk while mixing. Once mixed, zest 3 lemons and juice once and add to the mix. Mix a little more to incorperate.
  2. 2. Pour inro silicone cake mold and smooth over. Pre heat the oven to 140 degrees c.
  3. 3. Add to oven and bake for 40 minutes to an hour or until cooked through
  4. 4. Take out of oven and leave to cool for 20 minutes. In a bowl, zest half a lemon and juice it, add a little icing sugar and mix.
  5. 5. Use a tooth pick and knife to piece holes into the cake, then spoon lemon mix over in and let seep into the holes.
  6. 6. Mix water and icing sugar into you get a thick iceing glaze. Take your cake out of the mould and place on a board.
  7. 7. Drizzel over your glave then place into the fridge for 20 minutes to set.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Crack eggs into a seperate bowl then add so you can check there is no egg shells.

  • Stab through when you think its cooked, if its fully cooked your knife will come out clean.
