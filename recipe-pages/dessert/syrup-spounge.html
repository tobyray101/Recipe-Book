Syrup Spounge Cake

Home


About This Recipe

This syrup spounge cake is a classic British dessert, perfect for those who love a sweet cake treat. The cake is made with a simple sponge base that is steamed to give it a light and fluffy texture. The golden syrup adds a deliciously sweet and sticky element to the cake, making it a comforting and satisfying dessert. It can be served with warm custard or ice cream or just by its self.

Ingredients

  • 1 pack butter
  • 3lbs self raising flour
  • 1 1/2 caster sugar
  • 1 pint milk
  • 12 eggs
  • Margerine
  • Golden syrup

How To Make

  1. 1. Measure out flour and sugar and add to a bowl, cut your butter into cubes and add, crack your eggs onto the bowl. Measure out 1 pint of milk, start mixing and add the milk while mixing.
  2. 2. In a heat proof bowl, grease with Margerine and then pour some goldn syrup into a bowl.
  3. 3. Once fully mixed it should look like a thick cake batter. Drizzel on top of youir golden syrup in the bowl and fill to about 2/3 of the bowl.
  4. 4. Place a cartouche on top and then take a bit bit of foil, take 2 parts of the midle and fold them on top of each other to make a vent. Place this on top of the bowl and scrunch the edges in to hold tightly.
  5. 5. When ready to cook, take a pot with some water in, place the cake in and cover with a toight fitting lid. Turn up the hyeat and steam your pudding for 2 - 2.30 hours.
  6. 6. When finished cooking, take3 off the foil and parchment and flip it onto a plate and take off the bowl

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Crack eggs into a seperate bowl then add so you can check there is no egg shells.

  • To make a cartouche, you can use baking paper, fold it over twice and cut a circle half the bowl length.
