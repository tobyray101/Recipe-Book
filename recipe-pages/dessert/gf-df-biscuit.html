Biscuits (Gluten free + Dairy free)

Home


About This Recipe

This biscuit recipe is amazing it uses completely dairy and gluten free ingredients but still gets you a melt in the mouth delicous biscuit that doesnt miss out on any texture despite being gluten free. The use of ground almonds gives it a slight nutty flavour but mostly helps with the texture. This recipe uses gluten free and dairy free alternatives, however if you dont need it to be you can use the proper ingredients that will still work.

Ingredients

  • 150g caster sugar
  • 3 medium eggs
  • 75g ground almonds
  • 340g gluten free flour
  • Dairy free dark chocolate
  • Vegan cream
  • Baking block

How To Make

  1. 1. First step is measuring out ingredients and add to a mixing bowl, seperate egg yolks from whites and add yolks to mixing bowl.
  2. 2. Using hands or mixer, work ingredients together into a bowl. Keep going until you get a ball of pastry.
  3. 3. Cut the pastry into 8 sections and roll out into long rolls. Refridgerate and chill for 30 minutes.
  4. 4. After chilling, preheat oven to 140 degrees c, chop up your pastry roll into pieces, 1 inch thick. Place of a baking try with, press lightly on the top of them then put into oven. Bake for 12-15 minutes or until golden.
  5. 5. Take out the oven and rest for 10 minutes to set.
  6. 6. Boil a pan of hot water, place a bowl over the top to create a bain-marie. In to bowl add your chocolate, baking block and a small splsh of cream. Melt and mix together to create a melted chocolate.
  7. 7. Use a spoon or piping bad to drizzle chocolate over biscuits. Let chocolate set then enjoy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • You can use the egg shell or hands to seperate egg yolks from whites.

  • When your pastry is mixed enough, the mixing bowl should look quite clean without ingredients still being stuck round the bowl.

  • When baking the biscuits will expand so make sure to spread them enough.

  • If you dont need these to be dairy free you can change the chocolate to normal and use butter and real cream.
