About This Recipe

This is a simple and delicious brownie recipe that is perfect for any chocolate lover. The recipe uses basic ingredients like butter, dark chocolate, brown sugar, eggs, vanilla extract, flour, cocoa powder, and salt to create a rich and fudgy brownie. The brownies are baked until they are just cooked through, resulting in a gooey center and a slightly crispy exterior. This recipe is easy to follow and can be addapted to make it more interesting by using different ingredients and flavours.

Ingredients

  • Unsalted butter
  • Dark chocolate
  • Brown sugar
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla extract
  • Plain flour
  • Cocoa powder
  • Salt

How To Make

  1. 1. In a bowl, melt 200g of butter and dark chocolate. Add 175g brown sugar and 1 tsp vanilla extract. Mix then add 3 eggs and mix again until fully combined.
  2. 2. Add 75g of flour, 30g of cocoa powder and a pinch of salt. Mix until just combined.
  3. 3. Line a baking tray with oil and baking paper. Pour the mixture into the baking tin. Add broken up chocolate and stir in. Pre heat the oven to 180 degrees c.
  4. 4. Bake for 28 minutes or until cooked through. Then leave to rest the cool.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Bake for a few less minutes for a gooey center and a few minutes longer for a dryer cake like brownie.

  • Use a knife to stab the brownie when you take it out to check its cooked through.

  • Let rest for 15 minutes in the tin before taking it out.
