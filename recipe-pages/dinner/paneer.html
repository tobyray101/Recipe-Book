Paneer Butter Curry

About This Recipe

This paneer curry recipe is a vegitarian dish thats packed with flavour. The paneer is fried to give it a nice crispy outside then the softer inside absorbs all of the lovely seasonings featured in this dish. I cook this curry with a special spiced rice which i feel brings the dish together and isnt help back by plain rice. I serve it with a naan, which is optional but always recomended with a curry, theres a few different flavour options of nann you could pair with this dish.

Ingredients

  • Paneer
  • Oil
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Pepper
  • Curry spices: Curry powder, ground cumin, ground coriander, turmeric, cinnamon, fenugreek powder and chilli powder.
  • Rice
  • Butter
  • Stock cube
  • Tomato paste
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • Salt
  • Cream
  • Coriander (for garnish)
  • (Optional) Naan

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to cut your paneer to your desired size. Now preheat a pan on medium heat and add oil, fry your paneer until edges start to crisp, take out your paneer and set aside.
  2. 2. Now dice your onion and add to pan, dice your garlic, ginger and pepper and once onion has softened add to pan.
  3. 3. Now start making the rice, in a pot on low heat add your curry spices. After 2 minutes add a knob of butter, let it melt while mixing it, once fully melted add rice and mix in so full mixed. Boil the kettle and add desired amount of water as well as a stock cube. Bring back to a boil, mix around to make sure stock cube dissolves then cover and turn down to low heat. Cook for 12-15 minutes until rice soft and fluffy.
  4. 4. While that's cooking add your curry seasonings and paneer back to pan and tomato paste. Fry off the tomato paste then add tinned chopped tomatoes. And a knob of butter. Leave to simmer for a few minutes.
  5. 5. Now add cream, mix in and cook then take off the heat. Heat a naan if wanted.
  6. 6. Once rice is cooked plate up, garnish with coriander and enjoy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • When making the rice make sure to mix in the stockcube while boiling and make sure it fully dissolves.

  • If possible mince your garlic and ginger as fine as possible.

  • When frying the paneer, do it over a high heat so it crisps up nicely quickly before overcooking.
