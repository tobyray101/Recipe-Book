Seared Salmon With A Parsley Velote

Home


About This Recipe

This is a elegant and impressive dish that is perfect for a special occasion. The seared salmon has a crispy skin and tender flesh, while the parsley velote adds a rich and creamy element to the dish. The new potatoes and asparagus provide a nice balance of textures and flavours, making this a well-rounded and satisfying meal. This recipe can require some practice or skill as it uses some advanced techniques, but is perfect for anyone looking to impress a guest. Its also a great recipe to improve your overal cooking skills.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Fish stock
  • Parsley
  • Dill
  • Neutral oil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Skin on Salmon
  • New potatoes
  • Asparagus

How To Make

  1. 1. Blanch dill and parsley including the stems in boiling salted water for 30 seconds, then take out and put straight into a bowl of ice water to stop the cooking process. Blend the dill and parsley with a neutral oil. Then put into a pan and bring to a boil. Once boiling take off heat and put the pan straight into an ice bath to stop it cooking and it turning brown. Finely strain using a cloth or coffee filter and set the green oil aside in the fridge.
  2. 2. Then you want to prepare your new potatoes by cutting them in half and boiling them in salted water for 10-15 minutes until tender. Then take off heat and strain.
  3. 3. Now you want to make a roux. For this you want to mix equal parts flour and butter, melt the butter first then add the flour in a pot over a medium low heat until it turns into a wet sand like texture
  4. 4. Heat up water in a new pan with a fish stock cube and another with milk, pour stock and milk equally into the roux small bit at a time and the next batch once its fully incorperated. Once youve got your half dairy velote at the correct taste and thickness, take it off the heat.
  5. 5. While you are making the velote, get a frying pan on medium high heat, once at tempurature add a neutral oil and then your salmon skin side down.
  6. 6. Once the skin has crisped up and the salmon is part the way through cooking, take it off the heat and onto a baking tray under a grill until finished cooking.
  7. 7. Finely chop a small amount of parsley and place on top of the potatoes and put them under the grill.
  8. 8. Chop the ends off the ends off the asparagus and cook in a pan of melted butter and water until a fork can easily pierce it.
  9. 9. Next plate up, the sauce should be warmed then on the bottom of the plate with the green herb oil drizzeled through it. Next pace your potatoes and asparagus in the middle of the sauce. Place your salmon on top of your potatoes.
  10. 10. Finish with some salt and cracked black pepper and use spare fresh parsley and dill as a garnish.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • 15-30 minutes before cooking, place your salmon skin side down on a layer of salt, this will bring the moisture out of the skin and help achieve a better seared skin.

  • When you first put the salmon down, lightly press down on it with a spatula to ensure it doesn't curl up and cook unevenly.

  • For searing the salmon use an oil with a high smoke point.

  • Whenever frying anything, make sure to lay away from you to avoid hot oil splashing on you.

  • The green herb oil can be made way in advance and can be kept in the fridge.
