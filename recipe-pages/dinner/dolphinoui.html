About This Recipe

This dish is a creamy baked potato dish. I usually use this dish as a side with something else or as a dish for party style dish. I like to pair this dish with either a burger or pie or as a replacement for chips. It offers a different creamy texture and flavour from other potato sides.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Garlic
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Cream
  • Milk
  • Cheese
  • Salt and Pepper

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to add equal parts milk and cream to a pot on medium low heat.
  2. 2. Peel your garlic. crush and add to pot. season with salt and pepper. Cut your potatoes into thin circular slices.
  3. 3. After simmering and mixing for a few minutes, add your potatoes. Cook for a few minutes then take off heat.
  4. 4. Take out potatoes using a slotted spoon and place to the side.
  5. 5. In an oven proof dish, layer potatoes on the bottom, then a layer of sauce, keep going until ingredients are used up.
  6. 6. Sprinkle grated cheese on top and place into into a preheated oven on 180 degrees c.
  7. 7. Bake until golden and crispy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Smash your garlic clove with the flat of your knife before adding to the dish to release more flavour.

  • Using the right size potato can help ith this dish, i recomend using medium size.

  • Using the righ amount of salt and pepper will really build flovour. Herbs can also be used to give it a different flavour.
