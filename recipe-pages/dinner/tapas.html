About This Recipe

Tapas are small spanish dished that are usually cooked for a table and shared. This recipe shows how to cook a few of my dishes that I include when i cook a tapas. You dont have to cook them all, any from this bunch depending on how many people and how much you want to eat. Some of the options are gambas pil pil, padron peppers, meatballs, potatas bravas and calamari.
