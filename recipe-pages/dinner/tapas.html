About This Recipe

Tapas are small spanish dished that are usually cooked for a table and shared. This recipe shows how to cook a few of my dishes that I include when i cook a tapas. This gives you a recipe to some of the tapas dishes i like to cook, so you can choose from these or add any of your own. This recipe will show you how to make gambas pil pil, padron peppers, meatballs in tomato sauce, patatas bravas and a few easy oven extras.

Gambas Pil Pil


Ingredients

  • Prawns
  • Garlic
  • Red chilli
  • Olive oil
  • Smoked paprika
  • Salt
  • Parsley
  • Lemon

Instructions

  1. 1. In a small pan add a generous amount of olive oil with a pinch of smoked paprika. Bring to a medium heat.
  2. 2. Chop up chillis and slice garlic and add to pan. Fry for 30 seconds to incorporate the flavours.
  3. 3. Add prawns with a pinch of salt and cook untill pink and cooked through.
  4. 4. Serve up and finish with some freshly squeezed lemon and some chopped parsley.

Padron Peppers


Ingredients

  • Padron peppers
  • Olive oil
  • Salt

Instructions

  1. 1. Heat a pan on medium high heat. Add oil, then add your padron peppers.
  2. 2. Cook for a few minutes on each side, until the peppers start to blacken.
  3. 3. Salt generously, then plate up.

Meatballs in Tomato Sauce


Ingredients

  • Meatballs
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Aubergine
  • Tomato paste
  • Tinned Chopped Tomatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Basil
  • Bay leaf
  • Sugar
  • Smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. 1. Heat olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add Aubergine and cook for 5 minutes, then dice an onion and add it to the pot with a pinch of salt. Cook for a further 5 minutes.
  2. 2. Chop garlic and add it to the pot. Add tomato paste and fry off. Then add your meatballs and fry off until outside starting to brown.
  3. 3. Add tinned tomatoes, basil, bay leaf, paprika, salt and sugar. Simmer for minimum 15 minutes, but the longer the better.
  4. 4. Remove bay leaf and serve.

Patatas Bravas


Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Salt
  • Smoked paprika
  • Ground cumin
  • Oil
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • Onion
  • Peppers
  • Garlic
  • Chillis
  • Bay leaf

Instructions

  1. 1. Set a pot of water on to boil. Chop up the potatoes into medium cubes and add to water, with salt. Boil until tender. Drain water and shake to fluff up the potatoes. Set aside in a bowl and let cool.
  2. 2. Get a pan on the hop on medium heat, add oil then garlic and chilli. Add diced onion and peppers, season with salt and smoked paprika.
  3. 3. Add tinned chopped tomatoes to the pan with a bay leaf. Simmer for 20-30 minutes.
  4. 4. Season potatoes with salt, smoked paprika and ground cumin. Air fry until crisp.
