About This Recipe

This recipe is probaly my quickest dish to make, its simple the only struggle can be making the bechamel but once you have learned to make that its easy. It makes a great work day meal as it quick and simple but still tasty and filling.

Ingredients

  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Passata
  • Pasta (commonly Penne)
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Basil leaves
  • Parmasan

How To Make

  1. 1. First you want to make a roux. To do this you want to add butter to a pot on medium heat to melt. Once melted add equal parts flower, mix together, it should have a sandy like texture when done.
  2. 2. Once it is done, take off the stove and then heat milk in a new pot.
  3. 3. Take your milk off the stove, addd your roux pot back on and slowly add milk bit by bit and mix. You can add the next bit of milk once the previous is fully incorperated. This is now a bechamel.
  4. 4. Add a pot of water to your stove and bring to a boil, add salt. Once boiling add your pasta, cook for roughly 8-12 minutes or untill 'al dente'.
  5. 5. Add passata to your bechamel sauce to make it a rose sauce and season with salt. Grate some parmasan into your rose sauce while its on the heat to melt it in. Take a small amount of the pasta water and add to your rose sauce. Then mix in your past in the sauce.
  6. 6. Plate up and top with a drizzel of olive oil, shaved parasan and basil leaves

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Have a ratio or roughly 20% of pasata to bechamel.

  • When making your bechamel, add your milk while warm to help keep a smother consistency.

  • When heating the milk make sure it doesnt come to a boil as this will change the flavour.

  • The pasta water has starch in it, so adding a small amount of this to your sauce can help thicken it up.
