How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to dice your onion and slice your leek. Heat up a pot on medium - medium low heat, add a knob of butter and a drizzel of oil. Add your onion and leak to pot, cook for a few minutes then add salt and a pinch of sugar.
  2. 2. Now you want to set another pot of salted water on to boil. Peel your potatoes and cut them smaller, add to boiling water and boil until soft.
  3. 3. Once your leeks and onion are caramelised, add 2 tbsp of butter and let it melt then add equal parts flour, mix together until you get a roux.
  4. 4. Now slowly add milk, mixing in until fully incorperated before adding more. Keep doing this until you have a bechamel sauce. Add salt, pepper and herbs. Add your chicken pieces and cook through.
  5. 5. When the potatoes are cooked through, drain potatoes and place into a bowl. Start mashing, then half way through add pepper, butter and milk. Finish mashing and mixing.
  6. 6. Pre heat your oven to 200 degrees c. Add your bechemel mix to an oven proof dish, layer your mash on top. Grate cheese and sprinkle on top. Bake until top goes golden.
  7. 7. Plate up and finish with chopped parsley

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • If you have time to caramelise the leek and onion for longer then cook on a lower heat and dont add sugar.

  • When seasoning your bechamel with salt, pepper and herbs, taste it to judge if it needs more of anything.

  • Before baking, run a fork across the top of your pie mash to give it a combed look, this will help crisp up the top when baking and look nicer.
