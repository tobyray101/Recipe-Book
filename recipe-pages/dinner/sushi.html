Sushi & Salmon Fried Rice

About This Recipe

This recipe will really make your girlfriend fall in love with you. Really works a treat every time I make it for het. Sushi rolls with the optional nigiri, sashimi and salad looks super pretty but tastes even better. It takes some time to make so plan to make maybe on a day off. This is a dish that takes a while to master but once you have, they will look great.

Ingredients

  • Sushi grade salmon
  • 1 Avocado
  • 1 Cucumber
  • Sushi rice
  • Rice vinegar
  • Salt
  • Sugar
  • Mayo/ Spicy Mayo
  • Nori Sheets
  • Soy Sauce
  • Sesame Seeds or Roe
  • (Optional)Salad/ Edamame

  • Skin On salmon fillet
  • Oil
  • Butter
  • Tyme
  • Cajun Seasoning
  • Rice
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • 2 Eggs

How To Make

  1. 1. Prepare and cut your sushi salmon into long strips for rolls and into nigiri and sashimi if youre having it.
  2. 2. Weigh out your sushi rice, then wash it 3 times untill the water is clear, pour out the rest of the water then measure out the correct amount and pour on. Leave to soak in the pan for 15-30 minutes.
  3. 3. Once soaked, measure out your other rice in a different pan and wash. Then cook both rices, bring to a boil then turn down to a low simmer and cook for another 12 minutes. Once done take both off the heat and leave with lid on to steam for 5-10 minutes.
  4. 4. while the rice is steaming in a new pan heat up rice vinegar with some salt and sugar untill hot, not boiling.
  5. 5. After done steaming put the regular rice onto a plate and leave to cool and air dry. And put the sushi rice onto a baking tray, mix in the vinegar mixture till its all covered. Leave to cool for roughly 30 minutes or untill cool.
  6. 6. while cooling, chop all of your veg, dice onion, garlic and ginger and leave to the side, slice avocado and cut cucumber into a long quarter for sushi rolls. Cover your sushi rolling mat with cling film.
  7. 7. Now the rice has cooled, place nori sheet shiny side down, cover it all in rice roughly 1-2cm with a slight overhang on 1 side, sprinkle sesame or roe onto the rice. Carefully flip, squeeze a line of mayo acrossthe width about 1 third of the way up the sheet. Place your salmon and 3 slices of avocado along that line.
  8. 8. Using the mat roll it up tightly, squeeze as you go. Then leave on the choping board
  9. 9. For hosomaki rolls, lay the sheet shiny side down, cover with rice but without an overhang. put a line of sesame acress the width about a third of the way up the rice and place the cucumber. Then carfully roll.
  10. 10. Repeat but with avocado. Place both on the chopping board.
  11. 11. Now for nigiri, wet your hands and take a small amount of rice, shape into an oval, place a slice of salmon on top. Repeat untill you have as many as you want.
  12. 12. Now sushi is done, heat up 2 frying pans for the salmon and rice, On a medium high heat, let the pan get hot, oil it and then place the salmon skin side down. Move it a little to stop the instant sicking then let it cook while putting a little presure on top to get an even sear. After a fe minutes flip and cook on the top for roughly 20 seconds. Then flip back only the skin side, lower the temp and place butter, tyme, garlic and cajn into the pan and baste the top of the salmon.
  13. 13. While the salmon is cooking, in the other pan, melt some oil, then add the onion, garlic and ginger and cook till soft. Then add the rice and stir fry for a few minutes. Push to the side and crack in 2 eggs, scramble them then mix into the rice. Add a little soy sauce.
  14. 14. mix some soy sauce, sweet chilli sauce, sesame oil, chilli flakes into a separate pot. Then pour onto the rice and mix in.
  15. 15. As soon as salmon is fully cook, take off heat and leave to sit on a plate.
  16. 16. Now to serve, cut the sushi rolls into pieces, plate up with some salad and edamame if you want.
  17. 17. Plate up the rice and place the salmon on top. Add pepper on the side if youre Toby and love pepper.
  18. 18. Serve sushi with extra pots of soy sauce and mayo

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Use the down time of rice cooking/ cooling to prepare veg and wash any dishes used so far.

  • Wrap your bamboo rolling mat in cling film before using, this helps with washing and rolling.

  • Wet your knife before cutting each roll of sushi. This lets it cut through smoothly and will help ook much better.

  • Sushi grade salmon is okay to freeze, so once cut freeze whatever youre not using for next time.

  • When searing the salmon skin side down, make sure the pan is hot before adding the fish. This will help stop it sticking to the pan.
