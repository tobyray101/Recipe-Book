About This Recipe

This is a delicious and hearty fish pie made with a creamy bechamel sauce, tender fish, and a fluffy mashed potato topping. It's perfect for a comforting week night dinner. This fish pie uses salmon and cod however you can swap out for other fish you may have or prefer. Fish like sea bass or basa also work well in this recipe. This recipe benefits from the flavours of caramelised leek and onion which add a subtle sweetness to the dish. The herbs and salt and pepper develop added layers of flavour. Its served with buttery garlic asparagus on the side for a fresh and vibrant contrast. This dish incorperates many veggies so its healthy as well as delicios.

Ingredients

  • Salmon
  • Cod
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Leek
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Kale
  • Courgette
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Potatoes
  • Cheese
  • Herbs
  • Oil
  • Asparagus

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to dice your onion and slice your leek. Heat up a pot on medium - medium low heat, add a knob of butter and a drizzel of oil. Add your onion and leak to pot, cook for a few minutes then add salt and a pinch of sugar.
  2. 2. Now you want to set another pot of salted water on to boil. Peel your potatoes and cut them smaller, add to boiling water and boil until soft.
  3. 3. After cooking your onion and leek for 5 minutes mince your garlic and add to pot.
  4. 4. Cook for a further 3 minutes and then add 2 tbsp of butter and let it melt then add equal parts flour, mix together until you get a roux.
  5. 5. Now slowly add milk, mixing in until fully incorperated before adding more. Keep doing this until you have a bechamel sauce. Add salt, pepper and herbs.
  6. 6. Cut courgette into small chunks and add. Add your cod and salmon to the sauce and cook through. As the fish cooked your should be able to use your spatula to break your fish into pieces. Add kale and leave to simmer.
  7. 7. When the potatoes are cooked through, drain potatoes and place into a bowl. Start mashing, then half way through add pepper, butter and milk. Finish mashing and mixing.
  8. 8. Pre heat your oven to 200 degrees c. Add your bechemel mix to an oven proof dish, layer your mash on top. Grate cheese and sprinkle on top. Bake until top goes golden.
  9. 9. When fish pie is almost ready, chop the stems off your asparagus, add to a pan with a knob of butter and some minced garlic, cook until tender.
  10. 10. Serve your fish pie with the asparagus on the side.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • If you have time to caramelise the leek and onion for longer then cook on a lower heat and dont add sugar.

  • Taste your mash while mixing, add more seasonings if needed and more butter and flour if to dry.

  • Before baking, run a fork across the top of your pie mash to give it a combed look, this will help crisp up the top when baking and look nicer.

  • You can take the skin off the fish if you prefer but if you it I wouldnt because of the added health benefits.
