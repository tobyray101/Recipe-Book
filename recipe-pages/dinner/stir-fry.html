Garlic Chilli Stir Fried Tofu

Home


About This Recipe

This chilli garlic stir fried tofu recipe is packed with flavour and perfect for a quick and easy weeknight dinner. The tofu is marinated in a spicy garlic sauce, then stir fried with fresh vegetables like broccoli, bell peppers, and baby corn. Served over steamed rice, this dish is both satisfying and nutritious.

Ingredients

  • Pepper
  • Brocolli
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Red chilli
  • Soy sauce
  • Siracha
  • Honey
  • Tofu
  • Oil
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Baby corn
  • Rice
  • Cayenne pepper

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to grate your tofu, then you want to pat it dry with a paper towel. Finely chop chilli and garlic, set some aside and put the rest on your tofu. Drizzle with oil and add salt, pepper and cayenne pepper.
  2. 2. Next you want to pot a pot of pater on to boil. While its getting to temp, cut your broccoli into florets and in half. Once boiling put broccoli in and when cooked take out and put straight into ice water.
  3. 3. Now put rice into a pot and add water, salt and pepper, and bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer for 15 minutes or until rice is tender.
  4. 4. very finely dice half an onion and add to a bowl, add the rest of your left chilli and garlic, then add soy sauce, Siracha and honey. Start to mix then add a small amount of warm water.
  5. 5. In a wok or large pan, heat oil over medium-high heat. Add the marinated tofu and cook for 3-4 minutes until golden brown on all sides.
  6. 6. Remove the tofu from the pan then add broccoli, pepper and baby corn, stir fry for a few minutes. Add your tofu back and your sauce. Cook for 2 minutes stirring the sauce through.
  7. 7. Plate up with your rice and serve.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Letting your tofu marinate for at least 10 minutes allows the flavors to penetrate the tofu better. The longer the better up to a few hours.

  • Using a wok or large pan helps heat evenly distributed and easy stirring.

  • Blanch the broccoli for 2-3 minutes, then putting it into ice water instantly stops the cooking process. The broccoli will turn a brighter green when boiled.
