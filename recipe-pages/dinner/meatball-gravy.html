Meatballs Mash and Gravy

Home


About This Recipe

This dish is just as good if not better than the famous ikea meatball meal, it does use the same gravy after all. You can use other gravy this is just my personal favourite. Its a simple dish that doesnt take much skill or time. It should be a very easy recipe to follow and get a great meal. Its filling and theres the option to add more vegis to make it healthier. Either way its not unhealthy but very healthy for the taste buds.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Rosmary
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Ikea gravy packet
  • Cream
  • Meatballs
  • Oil

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to peel your potatoes and cut them into smaller chunks.
  2. 2. Boil a pot of water, salt it and add your potatoes. Boil until soft.
  3. 3. Drain potatoes and place into a bowl. Start mashing, then half way through add pepper, butter, milk and rosemary. Finish mashing and mixing.
  4. 4. Pre heat a frying pan on medium heat and add oil and meat balls.
  5. 5. In another pot add 300ml of water and bring to a boil, add gravy packet, whisk continuously. Once fully incorporated, turn down heat and add cream and whisk in.
  6. 6. Your meatballs should be frying, keep cooking turning them every so often. Cook until edges browned and inside cooked through.
  7. 7. Serve the mashed potatoes on a plate and top with the meatballs and gravy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Use a fork to stab your boiling potatoes to check if they are cooked. When theyre done they will do in easy and slide strait off.

  • After draining potatoes pat them dry with a paper towel.

  • If you want some more veg you can boil carrots or finely dice onion to go in the gravy.

  • Use salted butter or add extra salt.
