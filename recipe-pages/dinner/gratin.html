Leak and Butterbean Gratin

Home


About This Recipe

This recipe is a great comfot meal, its creamy with a mix of crunch, saltyness and sweetness from the caramelized leek. Its a great way to get some vegis into your diet while still having a tasty meal. Its great all year round but I feel its perfect for colder months because of how warm and filling it is. Is also benefits from the ingredients being cheap or staples of your kitchen youll already have.

Ingredients

  • Potato
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Oil
  • Dried Rosemary
  • Leek
  • Broccoli
  • Tinned Butterbeans
  • Cheddar cheese
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Nutmeg
  • Thyme
  • Dijon Mustard
  • Butter
  • Bread

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all, set a pot of water on the hop to boil. Peel and cut your potatoes into desired roast potato size. Once boiling add to water. Cook until soft inside.
  2. 2. Meanwhile get another pot on medium heat and add butter, slice your leek and cut your broccoli into florets. Add to the pot and cook for 5-10 minutes. Until the leek starts to carmelize. Add salt.
  3. 3. Drain potatoes, add oil, salt, pepper and rosemary and cook in air fryer until golden and crispy.
  4. 4. In your leek pot, melt butter and add flour, to make a roux. Now slowly add milk, mixing in until fully incorperated before adding more. Keep doing this until you have a bechamel sauce, for this you want to leave it slightly thicker than normal.
  5. 5. Add your tined butterbeans, including the liquid. Now add a spoon of dijon mustard, some ground nutmeg and thyme. Cook together and mix.
  6. 6. Cut bread into cubes, drizel a little oil on and season with salt and pepper. Air fry for a few minutes until crisp.
  7. 7. Grate cheese and melt into your sauce pot.
  8. 8. Once everything is cooked, plate up and enjoy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • After drining your potatoes, with the lid on the pot shake them to fluff them up and get a better crisp.

  • When making a roux, you should mix in the flour untill you get a wet sand consistency.

  • You can use any bread, however I like a nice baguette or roll from a bakery.

  • Try not to add to much mustard, you shouldnt be able to taste it it should just help bring out the cheesey flavour. Dont add cheese until mustard is fully incorperated. Regular mustard can be used but dijon is recommended.
