About This Recipe

This is a thai style fish cake recipe that comes with rice noodles and veg. Its sweet and spicy that gives it an amazing flavour. This fish cake mainly contains salmon, prawns and new potatoes with somefinely chopped veg and seasonings to make it taste incredible. This recipe you can do a lot of the prep beforehand then just cook the fish cake and make the veg and noodles in 10-15 minutes so it perfect for a weeknight special dinner or date night where you dont want to spend the whole night cooking. This recipe is for 2.

Ingredients

  • Salmon
  • Cooked peeled king prawns
  • Baby potatoes
  • Red onion
  • Chilli
  • Spring onion
  • Coriander
  • Smoked paprika
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Lemmon juice
  • Flour
  • Panko breadcrumbs
  • Thin rice noodles
  • Pepper
  • Sweet chilli sauce
  • Soy sauce
  • Garlic
  • Thyme

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to get a pot of water onto the hob, add salt and bring to a boil. Boil until cooked through.
  2. 2. Next you want to steam your salmon, in a pan on medium low heat add your salmon, thyme, 2 garlic cloves opened, lemon juice and water to lightly cover the bottom. Place a tight fitting lid over and steam until cooked through.
  3. 3. While they are both cooking you want to cut your veg for your fish cake mix, de-seed and finely dice red chillis, finiely dice red and spring onion root end amd finely chop fresh corriander. Add all to a bowl.
  4. 4. Once potatoes have finished cooking, drain and move to a bowl, let cool down for a few minutes then peel. Use your hands to break into smaller pieces but without mashing it.
  5. 5. Once salmon is cooked, move to a place, remove skin and break up. Take your cooked prawns and and chop into smaller cubes. Add all 3 to bowl with prepped veg.
  6. 6. Season your fish cake mix with, smoked paprika, salt & pepper, lemmon juice and some sweet chilli sauce. Mix togeth but be cauful you dont mush the salmon or potatoes any smaller.
  7. 7. Next shape your fish cakes using a larger cookie cutter and place to the side. Now prepare a bowl of panko breadcrumbs and then in another add flour and water and whisk to make a liquid with a medium consistency. Season with salt and pepper.
  8. 8. Dip your fish cakes in the flour mixture then roll in the panko breadcrumbs and place on a plate. Put in air fryer and cook for 12-15 minutes, flipping for the last 4 mins.
  9. 9. While the fish cakes are cooking, prepare your noodles and veg. Add your rice noodles to a bowl and pour over boiling water to cover. Leave for 2-3 mins then drain.
  10. 10. Slice red onion and pepper, chop spring onion and add to an oiled pan on medium high heat. Stir fry untill soft then add soy sauce and sweet chilli sauce. Mix togther with cooked noodles then plate up.
  11. 11. Once fish cakes are cooked, plate up with noodles and veg.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Boiling baby potatoes takes roughly 20-25 minutes. Steaming salmon takes roughly 8-11 minutes, it will be a consistent colour through when cooked fully.

  • When shaping your fish cakes make sure to press down firmly to make sure it fills out the cookie cutter and sticks together firmly.

  • Getting the right consistency for your flour and water mixture in important as this will allow the breadcrumbs to stick. It should be thin enough that when you take the whisk out some will still drip off but if you put your finger in it will coat.

  • When mashing potatoes and salmon and mixing into bowl, I would recommend using your hands because this will give you more control over not over mashing.

  • You can taste your fish cake mix to see when seasonings you may need to add more off.
