Miso Salmon & Sweet Chilli Veg Noodles

Home

About This Recipe

This is a great recipe the is healthy both for your body and taste buds. It mixes the savoury and umami flavours from the glazed salmon with the sweet and spicy flavours from the fresh veg. Its quick to cook but really impresses guests and could pass as restaurant quality as well as looking the part.

Ingredients

  • Skin on salmon fillets
  • Miso paste
  • Rice vinegar
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Chilli flakes
  • Minced garlic
  • (Optional) Mirin
  • Vegtable Oil
  • Thin rice noodles
  • Carrot
  • Pepper
  • Red onion
  • Sweet chilli sauce

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to prep your veg, you want to slice your pepper and red onion and carrots you want to peel and cut into ribbons, set all aside in a bowl.
  2. 2. Heat up a pit of water and bring to a boil. Put your rice noodles in until done, should take roughly 3 minutes. When cooked, strain the water and run under cool water. Strain again and set aside.
  3. 3. Heat up a frying pan on medium high heat, then add oil and salmon skin side down. Fry for a few minutes untill skin is crispy, making sure the skin doesnt stick.
  4. 4. While thats cooking in a small bowl, mix soy sauce, miso paste, minced garlic, rice wine vinegar, sesame oil, chilli flakes and mirin if you have.
  5. 5. Take your salmon out of the pan when the skin is crispy, glaze your salmon with the mix you just made, then lay on a baking tray with baking paper and cook in oven for a few minutes untill cooked through.
  6. 6. Mix your veg with soy and sweet chilli sauce.
  7. 7. Plate up rice noodles first with veg on top then lay salmon on.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • When frying use an oil with a high smoke point, for exarmple a veg oil.

  • When frying the salmon skin, the salmon flesh should start to cook too, then when you finish it off in the oven, look at the side of the salmon to see how cooked it is.

  • When making the carrot ribons if you dont have the ribon peeler, you can use a regular peeler to get then strips then cut them to size.
