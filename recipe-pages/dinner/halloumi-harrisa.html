Halloumi & Harrisa Lentils

Home


About This Recipe

This halloumi harrisa lentil recipe is a slightly more unique recipe but is packed with flavour and nutrients. The harrisa paste adds a earthy, smokey spice to the lentils which pairs perfectly with the salty halloumi. The dish is topped with crispy homemade croutons which add a nice texture contrast to the soft lentils and halloumi. This recipe is super simple to make and is perfect for a quick weeknight dinner or meal prep for the week.

Ingredients

  • Halloumi
  • Lentils
  • Red onion
  • Cherry/ plum tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Spinach
  • Harrisa paste
  • Oil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Veg stock
  • Bread

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to soak your lentils in a bowl of water.
  2. 2. Pre heat a pan on medium heat and add oil and sliced red onions. Add a pinch of salt and sugar. Cook until soft. Chop up garlic and cheery tomatoes and add to pan, turn down to medium-low.
  3. 3. Slice your halloumi and pat dry with kitchen paper.
  4. 4. In a new frying pan on medium high heat add oil and fry off halloumi until golden brown, flipping when needed. Take out when done and set aside.
  5. 5. In a jug pour 300ml of boiling water and add a veg stock cube, stir until dissolved. Add lentils and harrisa paste then pour over your veg stock. Simmer until water reduced by half.
  6. 6. Next to make croutons, cut your bread into cubes, drizel over oil and season with salt and pepper. Air fry for 3-5 minutes until golden brown.
  7. 7. Add spinach to the pan with the lentils and stir through. Cook for a further 2 minutes.
  8. 8. Plate up, lay the halloumi on top with your croutons.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • You can oven roast your croutons instead of air frying them. will take roughly 10-15 minutes.

  • Soaking your lentils and halloumi will help them cook more evenly and give a better texture.
