Aubergine Lasagne

About This Recipe

This recipe is for a triple layer aubergine and veg lasagne with mince. This lasagne looks good but tastes even better, it has 3 layers of mince and veg mix, a layer of lasagne sheets and a layer of bechemel. Theres cheese sprinkled throughout, with a whole layer sprinked on the top to crisp up. This recipe can take some time as you have to cook in a pan then oven bake but its so worth it.

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Aubergine
  • Carrot
  • Pepper
  • Tinned tomatoes
  • Tomato paste
  • Mince
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Dried Basil
  • Veg Stock
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Grated cheese
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Sugar
  • (Optional)Red wine

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to prepare your veg. Dice your onion, garlic, carrot, aubergine and pepper. Heat up a large pot on medium heat and add oil then your diced veg. Cook and stir for 5-10 minutes
  2. 2. Add some tomatos paste, fry it off a bit and then add mince. Cook for a few minutes then deglave the pan with some worcestershire sauce. Add salt, pepper and and a tbls of butter. Let the butter melt in.
  3. 3. Once the butter has melted add tinned tomatoes, red wine, dried basil, sugar and a drizzel of olive oil.
  4. 4. While this is cooking start making a roux. In a new pot, melt better then add flour, keep mixing until you get a wet sant like consistency.
  5. 5. Slowly add milk mixing it in as you add slowly.
  6. 6. Pre heat oven to 180 degrees c. Layer your mix into an oven proof dish. Layer a third of your mince and veg mix, then you lasagne sheets then bechemel. Layer 3 times.
  7. 7. Sprinkle on grated cheese and cook in oven for 40 minutes. Plate up.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Soak your lasagne sheets in boiling water for a few minutes before baking.

  • Veg in this recipe like onion and aubergine benefit from longer cooking times so simmering the veg in the pot can help build flavour.

  • You want your bechemel to be quite thick aswell has having a lot so you want to put a lot of butter and flour whenmaking the roux.

  • You want to save the majority of your bechemel for the top of your lasagne and spread it out so it covers the whole top.
