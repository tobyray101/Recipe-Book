Seared Tuna Steak

Home


About This Recipe

This seared tuna dish is really one that will impress. Whether your cooking it for a date night, to show off your skills or just a treat, its full of flavour, presents amazing and doesnt take long to cook or prepare so it wont take you away from your guests for long.

Ingredients

  • Tuna steak
  • Sourdough bread
  • Avocado
  • Sliced cherry tomatoes
  • Mushrooms
  • Salt & pepper
  • Butter
  • Oil
  • Rocket
  • Balsamic Glaze
  • Lime juice

How To Make

  1. 1. Leave tuna to rest and get to room temperature. Season your tuna steak with salt and pepper.
  2. 2. Bring pan up to a high heat, add oil to the pan then lay your tuna. Sear both sides then take out and rest.
  3. 3. Heat a pan on medium heat, add oil and butter then add mushrooms and sautee.
  4. 4. When youre ready to eat, cook for a few minutes on either side on a medium heat.
  5. 5. Slice your sourdough bread and put under the grill to toast it.
  6. 6. While thats toasting, cut your avocado, place into a bowl, add salt, pepper and lime juice to taste. Mash and mix.
  7. 7. Plate up your bread in the middle of the plate, spread the avocado on top, place some tomatoes on the avocado. Place your tuna steak on top then top it with rocket and drizzel balsamic glaze over the top. Place mushrooms and the rest of tomatoes around your plate.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Pre heat your pan and get it right up to temp before adding oil. This will help stop the tuna sticking to the pan.

  • For searing your tuna use a neutral oil with a high smoke point. This stops the oil burning or changing the flavour of the tuna. A good exarmple of this would be vegtable oil.

  • Tuna steaks can be hard to find in supermarkets, I have found them in tesco and waitrose so they are your best bets.
