Jazzed up packet ramen

About This Recipe

This recipe takes something as simple as a packet of ramen and turns it into a restaurant quality meal. We first cooked this whist hung over the day after my birthday and it became a staple since. Its an easy and fun recipe with great results you'll definetly enjoy.

Ingredients

  • Ramen packet (spicy) or (sesame)
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Tenderstem Brocoli
  • Spring Onion
  • Bell Pepper
  • Soy Sauce
  • Fillet
  • Cajun
  • Sesame Oil
  • Chilli Oil (Spicy)
  • Miso paste (Sesame)
  • Eggs

How To Make

  1. 1. Heat pan on a low medium and add oil.
  2. 2. Cut onion and spring onion and fry off in the pan. After a few minutes cut and add garlic, ginger and brocoli.
  3. 3. Add spices that come with the packet and any others to taste. Season fillets with cajun and set to airfry for 12 minutes.
  4. 4. Deglaze the pan with soy sauce.
  5. 5. Boil kettle and add, turn hop up to max and add ramen noodles. Pour boiling water into third pan, turn to max and boil eggs for 5 and a half minutes.
  6. 6. Add thai paste, chilli oil, sesame oil, miso paste to the ramen.
  7. 7. Plate up, cut peppers, peel eggs and cut fillets and add to plate.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Switch out the brocoli with whatever veg you have in the fridge.

  • Put soft boiled eggs straight from pan to iced water to stop it cooking internally, and can be easier to peel.

  • Cut the veg small so you can drink the broth at the end without bits.
