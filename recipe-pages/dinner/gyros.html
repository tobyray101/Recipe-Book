About This Recipe

This halloumi gyros recipe is a perfect summer time dinner recipe. It always impresses and is pachet with flavour and nutrients. This gyros is served with hot honey halloimi, mediterranean roasted veg, crispy chips and a refreshing homemade tzatziki. Alternate veg can be used in your roasted veg these in the recipe is just what I recommend. The homemade tzatziki is super simple to make and adds a fresh and creamy element to the dish. It uses fresh herbs and garlic to give it depth and a mix af grates cucumber and chopped chunks to give it a nice texture.

Ingredients

  • Halloumi
  • Honey
  • Chilli flakes
  • Chilli powder
  • Flat bread
  • Yougurt
  • Cucumber
  • Olive oil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Garlic
  • Lemon juice
  • Mint
  • Dill
  • Pepper
  • Aubergine
  • Courgette
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Potatoes
  • Lettuce
  • Paprika
  • Thyme

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all pre heat your oven to 200 degrees c. Then you want to cut your mediterranean veg into medium chunks. This is your Peppers, Courgette, Aubergine, Onions and Tomato. Then place on a baking tray with parchment paper. Marinate with olive oil, salt, pepper, paprika and thyme. Place in oven and bake for 30 minutes or until tender.
  2. 2. Next you want to start your chips. Get a pot of water on the hob and bring to a boil. Cut your potatoes into chip size and add to boiling water with salt. Boil until tender.
  3. 3. Chop your halloumi into bite size pieces and place into bowl. Drizel some honey and add salt, chilli flakes and chilli powder. Get a pan on and bring to a medium heat. Add your halloumi and fry until desired golden colour.
  4. 4. By this time the chips should be done boiling. Drain water, place chips onto plate and pat dry with kitchen roll. Place in to airfryer and cook until crispy.
  5. 5. Now you should have some free time to make your tzatziki. I like to use some grated cucumber and some chopped small. Dice some more cucumber into small chunks and leave to the side. Use a micro plane grater to grate some cucumber and put into a bowl. Finely dice some garlic and add to bowl. Roll your mint and dill and finely slice and add to bowl. Add yougurt, olive oil, lemon juice and salt & pepper. Mix together well then add cucumber chunk back in.
  6. 6. Once everything has finished cooking, lay your flat bread on a plate and add picket lettuce, then layer chips and your roasted veg, then halloumi. Drizzel your tzatziki over the top.
  7. 7. Serve up with extra chips on the side.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • When frying the halloumi the honey will come out but dont worry about that it will thicken and hold the flavour really nicely.

  • You can optionally use store bought tzatziki if you prefer.

  • When rolling a chopping mint and dill, be careful and use a sharp knife to cut to avoid bruising which will impact flavour.

  • Stir your chip and your roasted veg at least halfway through the cooking proces to make sure it cooks evenly.
