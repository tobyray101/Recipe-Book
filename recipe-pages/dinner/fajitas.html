About This Recipe

This recipe can be perfect for a dinner party or for a week day dinner. For a party you can serve a big bowl of your fajita mix and let people build their own or for a week day dinner assemble first before you sit down for dinner. This fajita recipe is quick to cook, versatile and mixes fresh flavours from the homemade guac with the spice from your seasoned chicken and veg.

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Pepper
  • Garlic
  • Fajita seasoning/ chilli powder, paprika, cumin and dried oregano.
  • Salt and Pepper
  • Chicken pieces
  • Oil
  • Wrap
  • Avocado
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Lime juice
  • (Optional) Corriander
  • (Optional) Sour cream
  • (Optional) Cheese

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to slice your onion and pepper. Put onion into preheated pan with oil on a medium to med-high heat. Cook until they start to soften and add pepper. Add salt pepper amd fajita seasonings.
  2. 2. Deglaze pan with a splash of water, reduce the water down and then add chicken pieces. Dice garlic and add.
  3. 3. Cut an avocado and remove pit, place into bowl and mash. Very finely dice a quarter of an onion and place into the bowl. Cut cherry tomatoes into 4 or 6 and add to the bowl. Add salt and lime juice to taste.
  4. 4. Once onion and pepper is browned and chicken pieces are cooked, take off heat. Lay a wrap down and add your veg and chicken mix, add some of your avocado guac, cheese, sour cream, corriander, a drizzel of lime and some cracked black pepper.
  5. 5. Fold up your wrap and lightly toast under grill or in air fryer.
  6. 6. Assemble as many wraps as you want and enjoy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • You can also add some raw bell pepper when assembling your wrap, this will give it some crunch and a hit of freshness.

  • 2 wraps per person is usually a good filling amount for dinner.

  • Deglazing a pan is where you add an amount of liquid to your pan to boil off anything stuck to the bottom of your pan, which circulates that flavour back into your dish. This wont work with a non stick pan as nothing will stick to it.
