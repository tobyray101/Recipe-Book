Ingredients

  • Soft shell taco wrap
  • Mince
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Pepper
  • Black beans
  • Avocado
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Fajita seasoning
  • Passata
  • Lettuce
  • Cheese
  • Sour cream
  • Oil

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to slice 3/4 of an onion, add to a frying pan, after oil, on medium heat. Add a pinch of salt and cook till soften.
  2. 2. Mince your garlic, and cut half your cerry tomatoes into quarters. Add garlic, tomatoes and mince to pan and cook through. Season with salt, pepper and fajita seasonings.
  3. 3. Add black beans and passata and simmer. While thats simmering, cut and mash an avocado into a bowl, finely dice the rest of your onion and dice your tomatoes and add to bowl with avocado. Season with salt and mix.
  4. 4. In your wrap lay sour cream and lettuce the spoon on your veg and mince. sprinke on some cheese and close.
  5. 5. In a new plan turn on to medium high heat and add oil. Crisp up your taco and flip. Take out, open up and add guac. Repeat with next taco and enjoy.
  6. 6. Repeat with next taco and enjoy.
