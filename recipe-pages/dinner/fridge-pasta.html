Fridge Raid Pasta

Home


About This Recipe

This fridge pasta is a great way to use up any vegetables you have in the fridge that need to be used. I often use this recipe just before a holiday or if I have other meals planned already to try not waste ingredients. Its a quick and easy recipe that can be made in under 30 minutes. The creamy tomato sauce is made with passata and cream, making it a comforting and delicious meal. This recipe is versatile and can be adapted to whatever vegetables you have on hand. Not all veg works with this recipe but most would.

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Any veg from fridge that needs to be used
  • Tomato paste
  • Olive oil
  • Passata
  • Cream
  • Chicken pieces
  • Cheese
  • Chiili flakes
  • Dried herbs
  • Pasta
  • Salt and Pepper

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all prepare all of your vegtables, dice your onion and garlic, cut whatever vegetables you have in the fridge into small pieces.
  2. 2. Heat olive oil in a large pan and add your onion and garlic, cook until soft. Then add the rest of your veg.
  3. 3. Add your tomato paste and mix in and add chicken pieces.
  4. 4. After a few minutes add passata, and then cream and a pinch of salt, leave to simmer for 10 minutes or until chicken is cooked through.
  5. 5. Boil a pot of salt water and add pasta. Cook for roughly 10 minutes or until al dente.
  6. 6. Drain pasta and mix with sauce. Save some of the water from your pasta pot and add it to sauce.
  7. 7. Plate up and sprinkle cheese, chilli flakes and dried herbs.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Salt throughout the cooking process to taste.

  • Some vegtables you could use are carrots, peppers, mushrooms, courgette, broccoli tommatoes, chillis, leafy greens, aubergine, peas, squash etc.

  • Add pasta water to sauce can thicken it and can change the way the sauce feels in your mouth.
