About This Recipe

This pizza recipe is a a recipe that takes all day but its so worth it. Its a great recipe for the weekend when your available in the day. This recipe is for 2 pizzas but you can adapt the measurement to make for more people. The dough is left to rise for a long time which gives it a great texture and flavour. You can add any toppings of your choice to make it your own. Some options I like are pepperoni, pepper, red onion, capers and olives.

Ingredients

  • 360g flour
  • 144ml water
  • Thyme
  • Half tbsp honey
  • Half tsp sugar
  • Salt and pepper
  • Yeast
  • Flour
  • Toppings of choice
  • Tomato paste
  • Pasata
  • Herbs
  • Oil
  • Cheese

How To Make

  1. 1. Start your yeast mix in the morning. Boil the kettle and pour some into a bowl, let cool down then add yeast sachet and a little flour. Mix together until you have a thick mixed liquid. Cover and leave to rest.
  2. 2. A few hours later at lunch time, add 360g flour, 144ml water, thyme, half tbsp honey, half tsp sugar, salt and pepper. Mix together until you get a dogh, cover again and leave until dinner.
  3. 3. When your ready to eat. In a pot fry of some tomato paste, add pasata a pinch of salt and some herbs. Mix together and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. 4. While thats cooking roll out your dough. Flour your surface and roll out the dough to your desired thickness. Place onto baking tray or pizza tray.
  5. 5. Spoon on tomato sauce on and spread evenly. leaving a ring round the edge for the crust. Grate cheese, sprinkle on and then cut and add any toppings of choice.
  6. 6. Pre heat oven to 200 degrees Celsius.
  7. 7. Bake for 15-20 minutes until golden brown.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Cook on a pizza try with holes in the bottom to make sure the bottom crisps too.

  • The less water in the dough the better so if you dont need the whole amount of water in the recipe, reduce it to make a more manageable dough.

  • Start the yeast in the morning, then the dough lunch time and then make for dinner time to give everthing enough time to proof.
