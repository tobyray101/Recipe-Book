About This Recipe

This roasted veg pasta can take a little bit of time to cook as you have to roast your veg, but it is low effort and very tasty. It can be great for a work night meal for you and your partner. The roasting of the veg enhances the flavour with the blended cashews adding a cremier texture with the finishing chilli flakes to add a slight kick. Its a great well rounded meal that you can have with a protein or not.

Ingredients

  • Red pepper
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Olive oil
  • Cashews
  • Pasta
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Dried mixed herbs
  • Cheese
  • Chilli flakes
  • (Optional) Basil

How To Make

  1. 1. Preheat oven to 200 degrees celcius. Peel onion and cut into quarters, cut pepper into quarters and de-seed, cut the top end of a full garlic clove off, cut regular tomatoes in quarters and leave cerry tomatoes whole.
  2. 2. Place all on a baking tray and heavily oil, place garlic face down. Add salt, pepper and dried herbs on top of veg. Bake until roasted.
  3. 3. Blend up a few cashews in a blender until its a powder and then set aside.
  4. 4. Boil a pot of water and salt. Add pasta and cook for roughly 12 minutes or until fully cooked. Drain out water from the pot but save a ladels worth and set aside.
  5. 5. When your roasted veg is fulley roasted, it should be shoft and edges blackened. Squeeze out the garlic gloves into a blender and add the rest of the roasted veg. Blend until liquidy, then add your blended cashews and a splash of olive oil and then blend again.
  6. 6. Next put your sauce into a pan then add your pasta water and pasta. Mix all togehter and plate up.
  7. 7. Finish with a garnish of grated cheese, chilli flakes and optional basil leaf.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Use a blender with an open top or hole as you are blending hot ingredients.

  • The water left after cooking water is salty and has starch that has come off the pasta while cooking. Adding a small omout of this to your sauce will thicken it.

  • Olive oil adds a richness to the sauce. A good quality olive oil can add more flavour.
