Shredded Tofu Crunch Wrap

Home


About This Recipe

This recipe is super simple and quick to make, I never liked tofu untill i tried it like this and i cant get enough now. Its surprisingly filling, I usually have 2 and im full, no need for any other prep or cooking. Its combined crunch from the tofu and crisps with the fresh veg makes a great combo.

Ingredients

  • Tofu
  • Wrap
  • Lettuce
  • Pepper
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Mayo/ Spicy mayo
  • Soy sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Tortila crisps
  • Oil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Cayenne pepper seasoning

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to grate your tofu. Use a regular grater as you dont want your tofu too small. Then pat dry with kitchen roll and put into a pre heated and oiled frying pan on medium heat.
  2. 2. Add salt, pepper and cayenne to the tofu. Then you want to fry off the tofu untill it starts to get crispy.
  3. 3. Once its crisped up, add a tbl spoon of soy sauce and some tomato paste. Add a splash of water if tomato paste is clumping up.
  4. 4. While thats cooking prepare your veg, peel off leafs from you iceberg lettuce and cut if you want. Slice a pepper and quarter cherry tomatoes.
  5. 5. Now the tofu should be crispy and water reduced. Prepare your wrap, start with either mayo, then lay your veg and put some tofu on top. Crush up some crisps and lay them on top and drizzel more sauce.
  6. 6. Prepare both wraps and wrap them tight. Optional toast them.
  7. 7. Plate up and enjoy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • When assembling your wraps, place the ingredients in a line and fold the shorter edges in firsts.

  • You can toast your wraps under a grill or better in an air fryer for a minute if you have one.

  • Use a firm tofu for this recipe as it holds its shape better when grating and frying.
