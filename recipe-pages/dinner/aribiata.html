About This Recipe

This aribiata pasta recipe is a great mix of simplicity and flavour. It combines a basic tomat sauce thats packed with fragrant garlic and basil falvours with richness from the tommatoes and olive oil. Which is then incorperated with fried of chilli, garlic and chorizo. This revipe can be made vegitrian or not. The only hold back is whether or not you like spice.

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Olive oil
  • Tinned tomatoes
  • Basil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Sugar
  • Garlic
  • Red Chillis
  • Vegi chorizo
  • Chilli Flakes
  • Penne
  • Water
  • Red or orange pepper

How To Make

  1. 1. Heat up a sauce pan, add olive oil and fry off onions on medium low heat till soft.
  2. 2. Then add garlic and cook in and add salt.
  3. 3. Add tinned tomatoes then more salt and a pinch of sugar.
  4. 4. Add basil and olive oil, leave to simmer for 10 minutes. Remove from heat.
  5. 5. Add water to a pan and salt it, bring to a boil.
  6. 6. In a new frying pan add oil, garlic and chilli and fry off on a medium low heat. Then add your diced vegi chorizo. A few minutes later add your diced pepper.
  7. 7. Once the water is boiling, turn the heat down a little and add the pasta, boil for 12 minutes
  8. 9. While that's boiling, add your tomato sauce to the frying pan with the chilli and oil. Add more salt, chilli flakes and olive oil. Cook that together until pasta is done, then mix all together and add pasta water.
  9. 10. Plate up and enjoy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Olive oil adds a richness to the sauce

  • Salt throughout the cooking process to build up flavour

  • Finish off with a garnish of basil and chilli flakes for presentation

  • Use pasta water to adjust the consistency of the sauce

  • Adjust spice levels by how many chillis you use and whether or not you de-seed them.
