About This Recipe

This spagbowl recipe is a classic weeknight dinner. Its healthy, full of vegetables and tastes delicious. Its a well rounded balanced dish that benefits from the richness from olive oil and tinned tomatoes. This recipe can benefit from being cooked slowly to build flavour but can also be made quickly if needed. Once simmering the sauce it is very low effort and can be left until you want to eat. ANother benefit of this recipe is its great for batch cooking and having leftoverers for another dinner or freezing.

Ingredients

  • Onion
  • Carrot
  • Celery
  • Garlic
  • Tomato paste
  • Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • Mince
  • Sugar
  • Worcestershire sauce
  • Spaghetti
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cheddar or paresan cheese
  • Herbs and chilli flakes
  • Olive oil

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to prepare your ingredients. Dice your onion, carrot and celery. Season with salt. Heat up pan on medum heat and add olive oil then put chopped veg in.
  2. 2. Next dice your garlic and add it to the pan. After a few minutes, add the tomato paste and stir.
  3. 3. Add your mince and cook through. Once nearly cooked add the tinned tomatoes, sugar and Worcestershire sauce. Simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  4. 4. While simmering, boil water, salt and add spaghetti. Cook until al dente.
  5. 5. Drain spaghetti. Plate up spaghetti and add sauce on top.
  6. 6. Finish with cheese, herbs and chilli flakes.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Cooking on a lower heat for longer can help build flavour. You dont need to add sugar if you are cooking for a long time.

  • Add some pepper and cheese on top of the pasta, a little tip to make your dish more interesting and build your flavour profile.

  • When dicing an onion, leave the root, it helps hold the layers together.
