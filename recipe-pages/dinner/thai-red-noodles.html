Thai Red Noodles

Home


About This Recipe

This Thai red noodle recipe is a one pot dish, its quick to cook and clean up. This makes it a perfect week night dinner. This recipe is ideal because it uses a lot of veg that you probably already have in your kitchen. This recipe pairs the aromatic and spicy flavours from the red thai paste with the rich creamy texture from the coconut milk and the fresh and citrisy hit from the veg and lime.

Ingredients

  • Onion, white and green
  • Pepper & Carrot
  • Garlic & Ginger
  • Oil
  • Red thai paste
  • Coconut milk
  • Brown sugar
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Fish sauce
  • Vegtable stock
  • Cayane pepper
  • Rice Noodles
  • Chicken pieces
  • Lime and (Optional)coriander

How To Make

  1. 1. First you want to prep your veg, dice your onion, peeled ginger, garlic and pepper, peel and cut carrots into ribbons. In a pot, sweat down onions on a medium heat and add a pinch of salt. After a few minutes add pepper the rest of your veg.
  2. 2. Add your chicken pieces and cayane pepper, then add a small amount of thai paste and mix your veg round in the sauce, add some boiling water and veg stock cube as well as the coconut milk.
  3. 3. Now add brown sugar, lime juice and a dash of fish sauce. Leave to simmer and encoperate all the flavours.
  4. 4. Once reduced to a good level, add your rice noodles and cook for 6 minutes oir untell fully cooked. Plate straight up.
  5. 5. Garnish with optional fresh lime slice and a sprig or coriander.

A few tips to perfect the recipe

  • When making the carrot ribons if you dont have the ribon peeler, you can use a regular peeler to get then strips then cut them into ribbons.

  • Once you have added the coconut milk, dont bring back to a boil for the rest of the cooking process, Boiling can lead to the coconut milk spliting.

  • Using a spoon to peel ginger can be easier than an actual peeler I find.
