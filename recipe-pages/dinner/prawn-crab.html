Prawn & Crab Linguine

Home


About This Recipe

This recipe is surprisinly easy for such a tasty dish. Its a seafood pasta using crustaceans like prawns and crab meat mixed in a spicy chilli and garlic based sauce. Its folded with rocket to add a nice fresh and peppery flavour to the dish and topped with finely grated parmesan to add a salty and nutty finish. This dish is great for impressing guests or just a tasty midweek meal.

Ingredients

  • King prawns
  • Shredded crab meat
  • Rocket or(spinach/kale)
  • Sun dried tomatoes
  • Linguine
  • 3 Red chillies
  • 1 1/2 Garlic cloves
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Lime juice
  • Parmasan
  • (Optional)Parsley

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to get a pot of water on the hob, bring to a boil and add salt. When its boiling add your Linguine and boil for roughtly 10 minutes or untiljust cooked.
  2. 2. While thats boiling you want to make your sauce. Roughly chop your chillis, taking out some seeds but not all, chop your garlic and add both to a blender. Add a pinch of salt, a splash of lime and match the volume of chilli/ garlic with oil. Blend together until smooth and thick enough for a sauce.
  3. 3. Heat a frying pan on the hob to medium high heat and add oil. Add your prawns and cook each side for 2-3 minutes until pink and cooked through. Lower the heat to a medium heat and add your crab meat and sun dried tomatoes. Cook for 2-3 minutes until heated through.
  4. 4. Drain your linguine and add it to the pan with your prawns and crab. Add your sauce and toss everything together until well combined.
  5. 5. Add rocket to pan then plate up.
  6. 6. Finish with parmesan and parsley if using.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Butterflying the prawns by cutting them part way through the back will help them cook evenly and add a nice presentation.

  • Grate parmesan using microplane grater.

  • Adjust level of spice with how many seeds from chillies you remove. If sauce isnt thick enogh add more chilli and garlic annd optional add a few rocket leaves and blend to thicken it.
