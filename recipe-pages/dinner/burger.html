Burger With Cajun Chips

Home


About This Recipe

This is a simple recipe on a lazy day. It is much better if you have an air fryer, so i would recomend that, it really helps the chips crisp up. ts a mix of quick, easy, tasty and reasonably healthy. It is versatile and you can change the mayo for any other sauce or change the veg for others.

Ingredients

  • Vegi burger
  • Brioche bun
  • Iceberg lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Red onion
  • Mayo
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Oil
  • Potato
  • Cajun seasoning

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to put a pot of salted water on to boil, cut your chips to size.
  2. 2. Place into boiling water and leave till cooked through. SHould take 8-12 minutes. Once cooked, strain water and take chips out and dry. Season with salt, pepper and cajun.
  3. 3. Place into air fryer drizzle with oil and set to cook for 15 minutes or untill crispy
  4. 4. Place burgers into air fryer and cook.
  5. 5. Cut brioche bun in half and toast under a grill or on a pan. Peel a layer of iceberd lettuce, cut tomato and red onion slices.
  6. 6. Once burger finished cooking, essemble your burger with mayo on the bottom with your lettuce and then place burger and then tomato and red onion.
  7. 7. Plate up with your chips.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • No need to peel the potatoes for chips, the skin will add to the texture and bring health benefits as they are high in fibre and vitamins.

  • Shake chips halfway through air frying the moke sure none are stuck together and cooks evenly.

  • After taking chips out of boiling water, pat dry with kitchen roll.
