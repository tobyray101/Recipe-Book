About This Recipe

This recipe is a great treat of a breakfast. It uses minimal ingredients and is easy to cook. It is very versatile as it can be used as a sweets treat of a breakfast with a wide range of different toppings, or with a different choice of topping it can be turned into a savoury dish that can be made to be quite healthy.

Ingredients

  • 100g Plain flour
  • 300ml Milk
  • 2 Eggs
  • Butter
  • Whatever toppings of choice

How To Make

  1. 1.In a mixing bowl, combine the flour, milk and eggs. Whisk until smooth and no lumps.
  2. 2. Heat a medium pan over a medium-medium high heat. When up to temperature, add a small amount of butter to the pan.
  3. 3. Pour some mix into the pan so you have a thin layer covering the whole bottom of the pan.
  4. 4. Once the bottom is golden brown, flip the pancake and cook untill that layer is golden brown too.
  5. 5. Repeat step 3 and 4 until all mix is used, add more butter between each batch.
  6. 6. Serve up with your favourite toppings.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Some great topping ideas could be, golden syrup, nuttella, lemon and sugar, fruit or you could even make it savoury.

  • Swirl the butter around the pan while its melting to cover the whole surface of the pan.

  • I would avoid mixing using an electric mixer, manually whisk or you can use a fork.
