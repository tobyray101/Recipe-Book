Fried Egg sandwich

Home


About This Recipe

This is a very simple breakfast with a few ingredients. Its relitively healthy with protein and good energy for the start of your day. Its a warm breakfast so can aslo be good for winter mornings.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • 2 Eggs
  • Butter
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Oil
  • Sauce of choice

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to heat a pan on medium heat and add oil. Crack 2 eggs directly into the pan. Keep the yolks whole.
  2. 2. While its cooking put 2 slices of bread in the toaster.
  3. 3. Once the egg whites are fully cooked, flip the egg to cook the yolk, you dont want the yolks too runny as it is for a sandwich.
  4. 4. When the toast is done, butter your bread, season eggs with salt and pepper and put in. Cut up some cheese and put that in too.
  5. 5. Put any sauce of choice in and plate up.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Crack you egg on a flat surface, this helps get a cleaner break so your less likely to get egg shells in your food or split the yolk.

  • Use salted butter, it makes a big impact on the overal taste.

  • Different types of bread can have different benefits, white has a different taste often prefered but has more sugar so less healthy. Brown is slightly healthier option, or seaded bread adds texture but is also healthy.
