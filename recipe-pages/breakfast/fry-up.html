About This Recipe

This recipe can take some time to cook but its worth it, especially for a special occasion or a loved one. This is the perfect recipe for a birthday or anniversary ect. Its a warm and hearty breakfast/ brunch that gets the day started in the best way.

Ingredients

  • Meat free sausage
  • Egg
  • Oil
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Medium tomato
  • Button mushrooms
  • Butter
  • Baked beans
  • Potato
  • Bread

How To Make

  1. 1. First step, you want to boil water in a pot. Once boiling, add potatoes.
  2. 2. Once thats in, cut your mushrooms in half then in a new pot on medium heat add butter and then your mushrooms. Cook until they brown.
  3. 3. After potatoes are boiled, drain and then peel the skin off. Then grate your potatos and shape them into a hash brown shape.
  4. 4. Cut a tomatoe in half, add tomato, sausage and hash bown to your airfryer and cook. Hash brown should take roughly 10 minutes, flip withway though. sausage should take 12 minutes and the tomatoe you can judge by looks.
  5. 5. Heat up some beans in a small ramekin.
  6. 6. Heat up a frying pan on medium heat and add oil. Crack an egg directly to the pan and cook until the egg whites are fully cooked, Use a spoon to flick some hot oil onto the yolk to slightly cook it but still keeping it runny.
  7. 7. Toast a slice of bread and butter it.
  8. 8. Plate up and add salt & pepper to eggs and tomato.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Salt your potatoes after grating but before shaping them into hash browns.

  • Dont overcrowd your pan of mushrooms, use a bigger pan then the amount of mushrooms needed.

  • Use salted butter unles trying to limit salt intake.

  • Use a slotted spoon to remove mushrooms from the butter instead of straining so you can pour the excess butter into a bin instead of sink.
