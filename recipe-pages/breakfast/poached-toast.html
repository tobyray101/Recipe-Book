Poached Eggs & Avocado On Toast

Home

About This Recipe

This breakfast recipe is perfect for a weekend or holiday morning, its relitively quick to make but always impresses with the optional extras to really elevate it. Eggs on toast is a staple breakfast dish, but with the addition of avocado takes it to the next level with added flavour and texture. Poached eggs can be hard to master but with these extra tips, they will be perfect every time.

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • Bread
  • 1 Avocado
  • Butter
  • White Wine Vinegar
  • Salt & Pepper
  • Easy Lime Juice
  • Chilli Flakes
  • (optional)Smoked Salmon
  • (optional)Leafs/ Rocket

How To Make

  1. 1. Half-fill a saucepan with water and bring to a boil.
  2. 2. While its getting to temp, cut your avocado and place into a bowl. Add salt, pepper and lime juice and mash.
  3. 3. Now water is boiling add a splash of white whine vinegar, then create a vortex by moving a fork in a circular direction and crack eash egg into the water. Set a timer for 2mins 30.
  4. 4. Toast or grill your bread and butter it. Then evenly spread your mashed avocado across it and finish it off with chilli flakes.
  5. 5. Once the timer goes off, take your eggs out and onto a sheet of kitchen roll.
  6. 6. Carefully lay each poached egg onto your toast.
  7. 7. (Optional) Lay a sheet of smoked salmon on each egg, and add rocket to garnish.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • After adding the white wine vinegar, turn down the hopfrom max to medium high.

  • Use a spoon with wholes to get eggs out and let the water drip off.

  • The timer is set for a runny yolk, if you want less runny then increase the time cooking.
