About This Recipe

When speaking to friends, tiger bread seems almost unanimously everyones favorite type of bread. It takes a pretty standard bread dough then you spread on a paste flavoured with toasted sesame oil. This gives it that lovely golden brown colour with the crunchy, flavoured top. This bread, simularly to other bread doughs will take some time as you need time to leave it to rise, but it is so worth it.

Ingredients

  • Bread flour
  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Rice flour
  • Golden sugar
  • Toasted sesame oil

How To Make

  1. 1. In a mixing bowl add 500 g of bread flour and 1 sachet of yesat. Add 1 and a half tsp os sugar and salt. Mix together. Create a well in the middl of the mix and slowly pour 300ml of warm water. Mix together until fully incorperated and you start to have a sticky dough.
  2. 2. On a clean surface, add flour and then take your bread mixture out and lay on side. Knead well then add to an oiled bowl. Cover the bowl with a tea towel and leave in a warm place to rise.
  3. 3. After an hour put the dogh back onto a floured surface and knead more. Shape it then wrap in oiled cling film. Leave for 45 minutes to rise more.
  4. 4. In a bowl mix 90g of rice flour, 1 sachet of yeast, 1 quarter tsp salt, 1 tsp golden caster sugar, half tbsp toasted sesame oil and 90ml of warmed water. Mix together until you have a spreadable paste. Leave for a few minutes.
  5. 5. Once bread dough ready, spread your paste on top of the bread and then score it. Pre heat the oven to 180 degrees c then bake for 35 minutes.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Dont rush the time the dough is left imimportant because it will rise. It should double in size when left.

  • Use the heel of your hand to knead the dough, pushing it away from you and alternating hands.

  • Once the bread is fully baked you should be able to knock on the bottom and should sound hollow.
