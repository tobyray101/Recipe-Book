About This Recipe

This focaccia recipe is a simple and delicious bread recipe that is perfect for lunch or as a side dish. The dough is made with basic ingredients like flour, yeast, salt, and olive oil, and is topped with fresh rosemary for added flavor. The result is a soft and fluffy bread with a crispy crust that pairs well with a variety of toppings or can be enjoyed on its own. This recipe is great for beginners and can be easily customized with different herbs or toppings to suit your taste.

Ingredients

  • Bread flour
  • Yeast
  • Salt
  • Olive oil
  • Rosemary

How To Make

  1. 1. In a mixing bowl add 500 g of bread flour and 1 sachet of yeast. Add 2 tsp of salt. Mix together. Create a well in the middle of the mix and slowly pour 350ml of warm water. Mix together until fully incorporated and you start to have a sticky dough
  2. 2. On a clean surface, add flour and then take your bread mixture out and lay on side. Knead well then add to an oiled bowl. Cover the bowl with a tea towel and leave in a warm place to rise.
  3. 3. In your baking tray, add oil and then place your dough in the tray. Cover with a tea towel and leave to rise for another 45mins or until doubled in size.
  4. 4. Using your fingers, poke your fingers into the dough to create dimples.
  5. 5. Create a mixture of 3 tsp olive oil and 1 tbsp of water and season with salt. Pour this mixture over the dough.
  6. 6. Pick your rosemary then poke sprigs into the dough throughout the top.
  7. 7. Pre heat oven to 200 degrees c then bake for 20-30 minutes until golden and crisp. Drizzel over more olive oil and serve.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Poking the dimples into the dough will help it rise and give a good texture.

  • Use the heel of your hand to knead the dough, pushing it away from you and alternating hands.

  • You can use finer salt in the dough but larger grains of salt for the top.
