Blue cheese and beet potato rosti with whipped avocado

Home

About This Recipe

This is a great lunch recipe, it can take some time to prep at first but its freezer friendly. Its a nice well rounded dish that always impresses. The rosti paires well with the light whipped avocado and the blue cheese sauce is a great addition and uses mostly ingredient alredy in the recipe.

Ingredients

  • Golden new potatos
  • Blue cheese
  • Beetroot
  • Avocado
  • Sour creme
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cumin
  • Easy lime juice
  • Mayo
  • Milk

How To Make

  1. 1. Boil or steam the potatos.
  2. 2. While boiling, grate the beetroot. When potatos are finished boiling, let them cool and then peel.
  3. 3. Grate the potatos and salt them, add the beetroot and crumble the blue cheese into it
  4. 4. Shape the potato mix using a circle cookie cutter.
  5. 5. Heat up a pan on medium heat and oil it. Place the rosti into the pan and cook. Flip halfway through cooking.
  6. 6. While cooking make the blue cheese sauce. For this, crumble more blue chese into a bowl, add sour creme, milk, mayo and lime juice and mash and mix with a fork.
  7. 7. For the whiped avocado, cut up avocado, add sour cream, lime, cumin and salt and pepper to taste and blend.
  8. 8. Plate up and enjoy.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • When you put the rosti into the pan, press lightly to cook evenly.

  • You can make multiple rostis and freeze the rest to save time the next time you want them.

  • Heavily salt the grated potato, they take more than you think.

  • If the whipped avocado or blue cheese sauce tastes too sour, add more salt to cut the acidity.
