Crab & Avocado Tian

Home


About This Recipe

This meal is a great lunch option thats healthy and filling thats packed with flavour. The creamy avocado pairs perfectly with the fresh crab meat and the tangy dressing brings it all together. Its plated with a potato rosti rosti and a poached egg on top. Theres a lemon mustard dressing on the bottom as well as caramelised onion and sun dried tomatoes rounds the edge. This meal really impresses, it looks great as well as tasting even better. The only draw back to this meal is youll need 2 circular cookie cutters, one larger than the other to plate it correctly. This also benefits from a squeezy sauce bottle for the dressing, however this is only for looks so not needed.

Ingredients

  • 50g Crab meat
  • 3/4 Avocado
  • Lime juice
  • Salt & Pepper
  • 2tbsp Dijon mustard
  • Lemon juice
  • 1-2tsp Honey
  • Mayo
  • Oil
  • 30g Smoked salmon
  • 4-5 Baby potatoes
  • Onion
  • Sun dried tomatoes
  • 1 Egg
  • White wine vinegar

How To Make

  1. 1. First of all you want to get a pot of water onto the hob to boil. Once its boiling, add your whole baby potatoes, no need to peel before. Cook until soft then take out and leave to cool.
  2. 2. While the potatoes are cooling, you want to make the dressing. In a blender add the dijon mustard, lemon juice, honey, mayo, oil and salt. Blend together and you should have a thick but still pourable liquid.
  3. 3. Heat a frying pan over medium-low heat and add a little oil. Slice half an onion and add to pan. leave to caramelize, stieing every so often.
  4. 4. Once the potatoes have cooled down, you want to peel them, the skin with be soft enough to use your fingers or a small knife. Once peeled, grate into a bowl and season generously with salt. Using your larger cookie cutter, shape your potato mixture into a thick circle.
  5. 5. Air fry or pan fry the potato rosti until golden brown on both sides.
  6. 6. While the rosti is cooking, you want to prepare the crab and avocado mixture. First cut and mash your avocado. In a bowl, mix together the crab meat, avocado, lime juice, some dressing and salt and pepper to taste.
  7. 7. Add chopped sun dried tomatoes to your pan of onions. Get a new pot of boiling water onto the hop and bring to a boil. Add a drizzle of white wine vinugar and salt. Add an egg and poach for 2 mins 30.
  8. 8. Next you want to make your tian, with your smaller cooking cutter, lay a layer of smoked salmon on the bottom and up the sides, then spoon in your crab and avocado mixture to fill the cutter. Take tian out.
  9. 9. Now everything is cooked, plate up. Drizzle dressing on the bottom of the plate then place your rosti in the middle, your tian on top of that and poached egg on that. Scatter your onion and tomatoes round the edge on the plate.

A few tips to perfect the recipe


  • Taste the dressing and adjust ingredients as needed. If to spicy from mustard, add more honey and mayo. if lemon is overpowering add more salt and a fat like olive oil.

  • Press down when shaping your potato rosti to make sure its firm and holds when cooking.

  • You dont want to over boil your potato rosti as this will stop it crisping in the air fryer.

  • When taking the tian out of the cookie cutter, hold the side and press down pushing it into your hand.

  • Pat the grated potato and poached egg dry.
